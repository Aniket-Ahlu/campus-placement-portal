How The Recruitment Cycle Works

A structured four-step journey from onboarding to offer release.

1
Company Verification

Recruiters register their organization. Placement admins verify credentials before any jobs can be published.

2
Opportunity Moderation

Job & internship roles are reviewed by admins. Clear eligibility criteria (CGPA, Branch, Batch) are enforced.

3
Strict Gated Apply

Students see proactive eligibility feedback. The server verifies academic prerequisites on submission.

4
Real-time Pipeline

Applications advance from review to interview and offer with audit trails and 15s live status synchronization.
